## 1.Examination of the appearance and structure

**Process Name**: Examination of the appearance and structure

**Tools / PPE**: Marker,insulated gloves

**Procedure Steps**

图1

1. Ensure the battery packs and inverters are turned off, and all circuit breakers in the Nexus Panel are in the OFF position. 
2. Inspect the overall tray, battery packs, inverters, and Nexus Panel for any defects such as bumps, deformation, or 
3. scratches. 3.Verify that all screen printing and labels are correct and legible. 4.Check that all pins and fasteners are properly 
4. installed. 5.Ensure power cables, communication cables, and other connections are secure and free of looseness. 6.Confirm 
5. there is no water accumulation or dust inside the unit.

**Notes**

>1. Do not inspect while energized.
>2. Wear labor protection equipment during operation.
>3. Testing work must be carried out by two people working together.
>4. Operators must hold valid certificates to work.
>5. All subsequent operations must meet the above requirements.

## 2.Turn on the battery pack

**Process Name**: Turn on the battery pack

**Tools / PPE**: Flat-head screwdriver, Phillips screwdriver, insulated gloves

**Procedure Steps**

1. Remove the protection plate as shown in Figure 1.

   图2

2. Open the waterproof and dustproof cover of the circuit breaker.

   图3

3. Switch the circuit breaker to the ON position as shown in Figure 3. 

   图4

4. Short press the battery start/stop button to power on the battery; the LED indicator will light up orange.

5. For details, refer to the Discover_Helios_314aH_OperationManual.

**Notes**

> 1. Pay attention to the status of the indicator lights. 
> 2. After the battery is started, the DC cables and DC busbars are live. Do not touch any conductors.


## 3.Start the inverter

**Process Name**: Start the inverter

**Tools / PPE**: Flat-head screwdriver, Phillips screwdriver, insulated gloves

**Procedure Steps**	

1. Remove the inverter front cover as shown in Figure 1. 

   图5

2. Open the two circuit breakers as shown in Figure 2.

   图6

3. Switch the PV switch to the ON position as shown in Figure 3. 

   图7

4. Press the start button as shown in Figure 3 to start the inverter; the LCD screen will light up. 

5. For details, refer to the Sol-Ark_18K-2P-LV_UserManual.

**Notes**

> 1. Before starting the inverter, the DC side is already live. Do not touch any conductors.
> 2. After starting the inverter, the entire system is live. Do not touch any conductors.
> 3. The operation for both inverters is the same; start them in master slave sequence.
